Account & Data Deletion

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your MitaWise account and all associated data using either of the methods below.

To delete your account, send a request from your registered email address to:

mitawiseapp@gmail.com

We will process your request within 7 business days.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed from our servers:

  • Your profile information (name, email, avatar)
  • All expense and income records
  • Custom categories
  • User settings and preferences
  • Budget configurations
  • App ratings and reviews
  • All authentication tokens

⚠️ This action is irreversible. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

Data Retained by Third Parties

If you used Google Drive backup, the backup file in your Google Drive appDataFolder is not automatically deleted. You can remove it manually:

  1. Go to Google Drive
  2. Open Settings → Manage Apps
  3. Find MitaWise and select Delete hidden app data

Profile photos hosted on Cloudinary will be deleted along with your account.
