What is MitaWise?

MitaWise is a simple expense and income tracker for Android. Log what you spend and earn, see where your money goes, and keep track of monthly EMIs and bills — all in one place. Your data is stored privately on your phone, and you can back it up to your own Google Drive.

Features

  • 💰 Expenses & incomeAdd transactions in seconds with categories and tags.
  • 📊 InsightsMonthly statistics, category breakdowns, and your running balance.
  • 📅 Calendar viewSee your spending day by day.
  • 🔁 Recurring expensesEMIs, rent, and subscriptions added automatically every month.
  • 🔒 Private & offlineWorks without internet. Your data stays on your phone.
  • ☁️ Google Drive backupOptional daily backup to your own Drive, and restore on a new phone.
  • 🔔 Smart remindersDaily reminders and alerts before bills are due.
  • 📤 ExportDownload your data as CSV or JSON anytime.

How we use Google data

Signing in with Google is optional. MitaWise uses your Google name, email, and photo only to show your profile in the app. If you turn on backup, MitaWise uses the drive.appdata permission to save and restore a backup file in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive — it cannot see your other files. We don't store your data on our servers. Read the full Privacy Policy.
