Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2025

MitaWise ("we," "us," or "our") operates the MitaWise mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Name — your display name within the app
  • Email address — used for authentication, password recovery, and transactional emails
  • Password — stored as a one-way cryptographic hash; we never store your plain-text password
  • Profile photo (optional) — if you choose to upload an avatar

Financial Data

  • Expense records (amount, description, category, date)
  • Income records (amount, description, category, date)
  • Custom categories you create
  • Monthly and weekly budget settings

User Preferences

  • Theme, language, currency, and date format settings
  • Reminder preferences

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Precise location data
  • Contacts, call logs, or SMS messages
  • Microphone or audio recordings

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide core expense and income tracking functionality
  • Authenticate your account and secure your sessions
  • Send transactional emails (welcome, password reset)
  • Sync and backup your data (if you opt in to Google Drive)
  • Improve the App and fix issues

4. Google Drive Backup

We request access only to the drive.appdata scope — a hidden, app-specific folder. We cannot access your personal Google Drive files. You can delete the backup at any time through the App or via Google Drive settings.

5. Data Security

  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt (12 rounds)
  • Tokens stored in encrypted device storage
  • Access tokens expire after 15 minutes
  • All data transmitted over HTTPS
  • Rate limiting to protect against abuse

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. When you delete your account, all data is permanently removed from our servers. See our Account Deletion page for details.

7. Your Rights

  • Access and view all your data within the App
  • Export your financial data
  • Update your profile and preferences
  • Delete your account and all associated data

8. Children's Privacy

MitaWise is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
